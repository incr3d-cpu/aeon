# FOMO

The app at fomo.family. Social terminal for memecoins and other tokens. Launched May 2025. Founders Paul Erlanger, Se Yong Park, Prashan Dharmasena (ex-dYdX). Self-custody via Privy. Apple Pay / card checkout via Crossmint. Chains: Solana, Base, BNB, Monad, Ethereum, Robinhood Chain, plus perps through Hyperliquid for non-US users.

Cap table per DefiLlama: angel $2M (Feb 2025), Series A $17M Benchmark (Nov 2025), Series B $75M Index Ventures and USV (Jun 22, 2026), angels include Mark Pincus and Humam Sakhnini. Total raised $94M.

## What it did to flow

- Discovery moved from CT and trenches onto a leaderboard. Coins that never hit a followed wallet lose the marginal buyer. Position from imperooterxbt, 2026-09-18.
- Win rate, public Dune (Midcurver77), 292,531 Solana FOMO wallets, 90 days through mid-August 2026: 6.16% profitable, 88% of winners made under $100, median loss $120, combined losses $1.26B. Top displayed profits mostly unrealized. Source writeup: ponyo / Four Pillars, compiled at https://www.btcc.com/en-US/news/deep-article/11952
- Solana daily actives from about 1,100 to over 90,000 in the year into August 2026 (same piece). Company figure, repeated in September coverage: 68,000 first crypto buys via Apple Pay, about $25M.
- Robinhood Chain blow-off then unwind. TechFlow 2026-09-23: FOMO wallets on RH chain from 100k+ early September to about 47k on Sep 20; FOMO volume on that chain from near $400M to about $42M. https://www.techflowpost.com/en-US/article/34190
- Oct 6, 2026: Solana memecoin volume share 89%, Robinhood Chain 7% (SolanaFloor). https://x.com/SolanaFloor/status/2107451471008612715
- By Oct 8 the remaining heat is Solana social flow. RH Chain is a small slice of daily fees. The app is still the house.

## Trading read

The alert is the exit. First print is information, the clan's print is supply. Public PnL can hide a side wallet. Size for a bid that dies in days. The structural long is the house and the referrer, not the follower.

Separate from this app: FOMO Network (Ashley Ward) shut down May 16, 2026. Unrelated FOMO tickers on Solana, Base, and elsewhere are small and have rugged. Do not treat a CA named FOMO as this business.
